Weekly report 1, Zcash and Bitcoin Cash

What two networks did in the week to 22 November 2026, counted from our own headers and listeners. Every number names its window, and every gap in our data is listed.

How did Zcash mainnet run this week?

We counted 28,665 chain blocks and 120 orphaned blocks, 0.42 % of all blocks we saw, at our one listener over 12 November 2026, 21:36 UTC to 21 November 2026, 04:27 UTC. That is a lower bound, because a block that never reached us is not in the count. Of the orphaned blocks, 13 came without a header, and none was deeper than 1 block.

Over 12 November 2026, 21:36 UTC to 21 November 2026, 06:34 UTC we counted 28,970 block intervals from our own headers, heights 3,566,683 to 3,595,652. The median interval was 17 s and 4.73 % of the intervals were longer than 75 s.

How did Bitcoin Cash chipnet run since the activation?

By our own headers the one minute blocks started at block 332,451. Over 15 November 2026, 12:33 UTC to 21 November 2026, 06:31 UTC we counted 8,310 intervals, with a median of 52 s and a mean of 1 min 0 s. Of these blocks, 25.9 % carry difficulty 1, a testnet rule that lets a block through after a long pause, so they are part of every wait below.

The waits come from the time our listener first saw each block, over 17 November 2026, 19:46 UTC to 21 November 2026, 04:25 UTC, blocks 335,748 to 340,629. Header time is given beside them. The mainnet column is computed for the 600 s target, we have not counted mainnet yet.
