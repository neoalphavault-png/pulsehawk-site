Weekly report 2, Zcash and Bitcoin Cash

What two networks did in the week to 29 November 2026, counted from our own headers and listeners. Every number names its window, and every gap in our data is listed.

How did Zcash mainnet run this week?

We counted 20,884 chain blocks and 78 orphaned blocks, 0.37 % of all blocks we saw, at our one listener over 21 November 2026, 04:20 UTC to 27 November 2026, 05:28 UTC. That is a lower bound, because a block that never reached us is not in the count. Of the orphaned blocks, 6 came without a header, and none was deeper than 1 block. The first run of this week started 7 min 40 s before the last run of weekly report 1 ended, so a few blocks may be in both reports.

Over 21 November 2026, 06:34 UTC to 27 November 2026, 05:31 UTC we counted 20,569 block intervals from our own headers, heights 3,595,653 to 3,616,221. The median interval was 17 s, the mean 25.02 s, and 4.73 % of the intervals were longer than 75 s.

Which Zebra versions did our listener meet?

Of 604 connections to Zebra nodes over 27 listener runs, 492, or 81.5 %, reported version 7.1.0 or newer. Per run the share was between 50.0 % and 96.0 %. These are connections of one listener to nodes found through the DNS seeds, not a count of distinct nodes, and a release candidate or alpha of 7.1.0 does not count as 7.1.0.

How did Bitcoin Cash chipnet run this week?

Over 21 November 2026, 06:31 UTC to 27 November 2026, 05:31 UTC we counted 8,379 intervals from our own headers, blocks 340,761 to 349,139, with a median of 45 s and a mean of 61.44 s. Of these blocks, 26.6 % carry difficulty 1, a testnet rule that lets a block through after a long pause. They are part of every wait below, and mainnet has no such rule.

Where is our data incomplete?

Gaps in the Zcash listener, none, the runs overlap. Gaps in the chipnet listener, 2026-11-25 15:53:16 to 2026-11-27 05:36:39 UTC. The listener stopped after its run ended, because the start of the next run failed at the code host, and we started it again by hand. The waits above therefore end on 25 November, the header counts do not. Both listeners run in the cloud, on one network path each.

How we count is on our method page, under PH-M-001.
