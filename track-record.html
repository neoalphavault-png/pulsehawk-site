Track record

Every forecast we publish, and every comparison of our count with a published value, gets an entry here. Each entry links the place it was first published, with its date, and stays open until there is a counted result.

How is an entry scored?

  • In band. For a forecast, the counted result fell inside the 90% band we published. For a comparison, the published value lies inside the 95% interval of our count.
  • Outside. The result fell outside. For a comparison this is a finding about the network, which did not run at its published value.
  • Open. There is no counted result yet. An open entry never shows a result.
  • Interval score. For each scored forecast, the width of its band plus 20 times the distance by which the block missed a 90% band, after Gneiting and Raftery (2007). Lower is better, and a forecast inside its band scores its width.

The list has 6 entries, 5 in band, 1 outside and 0 open. Entries are never removed. A mistake in an entry is fixed on our corrections page.
