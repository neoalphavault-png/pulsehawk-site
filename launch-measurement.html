Testnet and mainnet launch measurement

The first week after a launch shows how a network holds up under real load. We measure that week on our own nodes and set it against the week before, so your mainnet launch metrics come with a baseline.

What happens in the launch week?

  1. Before. Our nodes run from the week before launch and record a baseline.
  2. Launch. We count through the first week after launch, block by block.
  3. After. You get a before and after report with charts, the CSV and the method version.
  4. Publication. Results are published as measured, after you have seen them.

What do we count?

Blocks per second and per minute, the block time distribution, transactions per minute, confirmation time, fees, stale and orphaned blocks and the delay between our nodes. The full list is in our method.

For a new chain the week before is the last testnet week. For a testnet stress test measurement it is the quiet week before the test.

Questions we get asked

Can you measure our testnet stress test?

Yes. We watch the stress test from our own nodes and count what reaches the network. The load comes from your test, our part is the count.

How early should we book?

Two weeks before launch is enough for most networks. That leaves time to sync our nodes and record the week before.

What if the launch date moves?

The measurement moves with it. The price stays the same as long as the scope does.
