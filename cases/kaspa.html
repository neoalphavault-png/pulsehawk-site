Kaspa network measurements

Kaspa is the network we have followed longest, every day since August 2026, through our media brand Kaspa Pulse at kaspapulse.com. Every number on this page carries its date and is marked either measured, from our own nodes, or source, a series we take over and name.

How fast does Kaspa produce blocks?

Kaspa runs at 10 blocks per second since the Crescendo upgrade in 2025. At that rate a block time distribution is counted in tenths of a second, and the longest gap matters more than the median.

Source kaspapulse.com, Kaspa hashrate page, protocol block rate, checked 16 October 2026.

How do fees compare with new issuance?

In the 30 days from 12 September to 11 October 2026, Kaspalytics counted 68,493 KAS in transaction fees. In the same 30 days the emission schedule created 55,848,455 new KAS, about 815 times the fees.

Source Kaspalytics for the fees, a series taken over in the Kaspa Pulse daily log of 12 October 2026, raw data. New coins from the emission schedule, arithmetic, not a count.

Where can I follow Kaspa?

Daily numbers, the weekly reading and the cheat sheet are on kaspapulse.com. PulseHawk measures Kaspa under the same rules as every other network.
