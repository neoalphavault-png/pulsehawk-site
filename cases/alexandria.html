What did we review?

We worked from four sources.

  • The reviewer brief from the Alexandria team, with the hashes it names for AP-0030 and AP-0031.
  • The two records AP-0030 and AP-0031.
  • The public walkthrough video of 10 October 2026.
  • The public app, which we used on 12 October 2026.

What did we confirm on 12 October 2026?

  • Downloads match the brief. AP-0030 and AP-0031, downloaded on 12 October 2026, matched the hashes in the reviewer brief byte for byte.
  • Hashing stays on the device. The app hashes a file locally in the browser, and the file does not leave the device. Checked 12 October 2026.
  • New versions are clean. A new version creates v2 cleanly, and v1 stays unchanged. Checked 12 October 2026.
  • No fork at the same place. A second version at the same place is blocked, so a record does not fork. Checked 12 October 2026.

Checked by PulseHawk on 12 October 2026, in the public app and with the files we downloaded that day.

What was still open on 12 October 2026?

  • Same file, new title. The same file with a different title was accepted as a new original entry, without a warning. Observed 12 October 2026.
  • The operator is the trust anchor. Until a record is anchored, trust rests with the operator of the service. Observed 12 October 2026.
  • ALX-KASPA-1 is not specified in public. There was no published specification and no test vector for ALX-KASPA-1. Observed 12 October 2026.
  • Anchoring for entries of others. The app offered to anchor entries that other people had created. Observed 12 October 2026.
  • Archive standards and a second store. The archive standards followed and a second storage location were not named. Observed 12 October 2026.

Observed by PulseHawk on 12 October 2026. Points the team may have changed since are not checked again on this page.

How far has the project come, according to the team?

On 1 November 2026 Kas Knight told us, for the Alexandria team, that a first anchoring path on the Kaspa testnet TN10 has been tested. There is no anchoring transaction on the chain yet.

This is the team's statement, and we have not checked it. We will check the first anchor independently as soon as it is on the chain, and date that check here.

Statement of the Alexandria team, 1 November 2026, not checked by PulseHawk.

Is AP-0031 anchored?

No. As of 1 November 2026, the date of this page, AP-0031 is not anchored. Until a record is anchored, trust in it rests with the operator.
