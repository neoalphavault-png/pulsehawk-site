Independent blockchain measurement under load

PulseHawk measures how blockchains behave under load. We measure ourselves, with our own nodes or our own header clients, never from third party dashboards. We count blocks, transactions and confirmation times in a fixed window and publish dated numbers, raw data and an open method, for foundations, core teams and grant committees across all chains.

For foundations and grant committees

Ongoing measurement of a network works best as a public good. When a grant funds it, every result and its raw data are published openly, for the whole ecosystem, with the same method and the same rules as paid work.

A grant pays for the measurement, never for the result. The rules that make this hold are on our independence page.

How ongoing measurement works

What does a blockchain performance measurement look like?

A short summary, the measurement window in UTC, charts, a table with median, p90 and p99, the method version and a CSV with every block we counted. Every number carries its date.
