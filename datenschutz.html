3. Analytics with Plausible, without cookies

We count visits with Plausible Analytics, a service of Plausible Insights OÜ, Västriku tn 2, 50403 Tartu, Estonia. Plausible runs on servers in the EU, sets no cookies and stores no personal data. Your IP address is only used to form a daily changing anonymous identifier and is not stored. No data is shared with third parties or used for advertising, and visitors cannot be identified or followed across websites.

We count page views and a few events, a click on See a sample report, a download of a PDF or CSV file, a sent contact form and a click on a link to another website. Legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are used. More in the Plausible data policy.

Legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR, steps prior to entering into a contract, and Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR. We delete the data when the request is closed and no retention duty applies. More in the Brevo privacy policy.
