Zcash NU7 upgrade report

PulseHawk Upgrade Report PH-UR-2026-001, method v1.0. We measure Zcash NU7 (ZIP 259) on mainnet from 24 hours before to 72 hours after block 3,543,405, the activation height, with seven fixed values and no grades.

What should you keep in mind?

  • Our watch hung. From about 02:50 to 08:11 UTC on 6 November 2026 the watch saw no new blocks, because the DNS seeds could not be resolved from its host. The block pause alarm in the log from 03:04 to 08:15 UTC comes from that, not from the chain. M1, M2 and M6 come from the heads and are not affected. M3 and M4 come from the watch log and may miss tip switches in those hours.
  • Probes. The peer probes reached 28, 12, 6 and 8 peers. The shares in M5 are shares of these few reachable peers, not of all nodes.
  • Forecast. The M7 rows use our forecast of 4 November 2026 with its 90% band. Block 3,543,405 came 6 min 56 s earlier than that forecast, inside the band.

Reproduce this

The code is in github.com/pulsehawk/methods. These three commands fetch it, count again and compare with the published values.

git clone https://github.com/pulsehawk/methods && cd methods
python3 upgrade_report/report_v1.py --config upgrade_report/configs/zcash-mainnet-nu7.json --heads data/zcash-mainnet-nu7/heads.csv.gz --raw data/zcash-mainnet-nu7/raw --out out
cd out && sha256sum -c ../data/zcash-mainnet-nu7/expected/report-v1-zcash-mainnet-nu7.sha256
