M1 Block intervals

Not yet counted. The numbers follow after block 3,543,405, from our own count.

M5 Peers on pre-upgrade software

The share of peers in our sample that run pre-upgrade software at activation, 24 hours later and 72 hours later, with n.

Not yet counted. The numbers follow after block 3,543,405, from our own count.

M6 Time to stable

The time from the activation block until the mean of the block intervals over the last hour lies within 20 % of the new target and stays there for 60 minutes without a break.

Not yet counted. The numbers follow after block 3,543,405, from our own count.

Its time and the gap to our own estimate published before activation.

Our estimate before activation is 6 November 2026, 02:39 UTC, computed from our count of the block pace, with 9 in 10 of our draws between 6 November 2026, 00:28 UTC and 6 November 2026, 05:00 UTC, as of 2 November 2026.
