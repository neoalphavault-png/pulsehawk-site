The Bitcoin cycle bottom, how long after the top

In the three completed four year cycles the bottom came 410, 364 and 376 days after the top. That is a spread of about six weeks. This page counts the days and makes no forecast, which is the one thing that separates it from most of what you will find on this question.

Why you will see other numbers

The bottom dates for 2015 and 2018 are agreed on almost everywhere. The 2022 low is not. Some sources put it on 10 November 2022 and others on 21 November 2022, depending on the exchange and on whether intraday lows or daily closes are used.

Take the earlier date and that cycle ran 365 days instead of 376. The useful part is that it barely matters. Either way all three completed cycles land between 364 and 410 days. A range that survives a change of source is worth more than a single number that does not.

Bottom prices are marked "about" on purpose. Different venues printed lows within a few percent of each other on the same day, and pretending otherwise would be false precision.

What this does not tell you

It does not tell you when this cycle bottoms. Three cycles are three data points, they differ by 46 days, and the market they trade in is not the market of 2015. Search this question and most results will name you a month. We will not, and that is deliberate.

It also does not tell you a price. We publish no price targets, in any format, ever. The bottom prices in the table are history, not a template.

How we count

Calendar days between the top date and the bottom date, in UTC, with the top day counted as day zero. Top dates match the ones used on our halving to top page, so the two pages can be read together. Bottom dates and prices come from published cycle low data, cross checked against CoinGecko research and Bitcoin Magazine's bear market record.

Questions people ask

How long does it take Bitcoin to go from top to bottom?

410, 364 and 376 days in the three completed cycles. All three sit between 364 and 410 days.

When was the last cycle top?

6 October 2025, at 126,198 US dollars.

When did Bitcoin bottom in previous cycles?

14 January 2015, 15 December 2018 and 21 November 2022.

Will the next bottom follow the same pattern?

Nobody knows, and this page does not guess. What it means is your call.

Why do sources disagree on the 2022 low?

Different exchanges and different use of closes against intraday lows. See the section above. It moves that one count from 376 to 365.

Where do the numbers come from?

Every date is in the table. You can check each one against any price chart in a couple of minutes, and that is the point.
