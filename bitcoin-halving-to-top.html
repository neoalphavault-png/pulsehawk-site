The Bitcoin 4 year cycle, from halving to top

In the three completed four year cycles the top came 367, 525 and 548 days after the halving. The current cycle topped on day 534, on 6 October 2025. Every date below comes from the same source, so the four cycles can actually be compared.

Why you will see other numbers

Search this question and you will find 366, 367 and 368 for the first cycle, and 525 or 526 for the second. Almost nobody explains the difference, so here it is.

The top date is not a fact, it is a reading. Bitcoin trades on many venues at once. The 2017 peak lands on 16 December on CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap, and on 17 December on sites that read a different exchange. One day of difference moves the count by one. The same applies to the 2013 peak.

The halving date can shift too. The 2012 halving crossed midnight UTC, which is why some sources date it 27 November and others 28 November.

Our rule is simple. One source for all four rows, named on the page. A table that mixes sources produces numbers that look precise and cannot be compared.

What this does not tell you

It does not tell you when the next top comes. Three completed cycles are three data points, and one of them sits five months away from the other two. Anyone who turns four numbers into a date is guessing with extra steps.

It also does not tell you anything about price. We publish no price targets, in any format, ever. This page counts days between two dates that already happened.

How we count

Calendar days between the halving date and the cycle top date, both in UTC. The halving day itself is day zero, so a top on the next day would be day one. Halving dates come from the block at which the reward changed. Top dates and prices come from daily close data as published by CoinMarketCap, cross checked against CoinGecko research.

The live counters at the top of the page run from 20 April 2024 and 6 October 2025. They need no data source, which is why they cannot go stale or go wrong.

Questions people ask

How long is the Bitcoin 4 year cycle from halving to top?

367 days in the 2012 cycle, 525 in the 2016 cycle, 548 in the 2020 cycle and 534 in the 2024 cycle.

How many days after the halving does Bitcoin peak?

There is no single number. The four cycles so far peaked on day 367, 525, 548 and 534. The last three sit between 525 and 548.

When was the 2024 Bitcoin halving?

20 April 2024, at block 840,000. The block reward fell from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC.

When did Bitcoin top in this cycle?

6 October 2025, at 126,198 US dollars. There was an earlier high of 124,128 dollars on 14 August 2025, which several older articles still list as the cycle top.

Why do different sites give different day counts?

Because they use different top dates, and sometimes a different halving date. See the section above. The gap is one or two days, never more.

Does the day count predict the next top?

No. It is a count of what happened. What it means is your call.
