Upgrade readiness and network health

Before an upgrade we measure whether the network is ready. Around activation we keep watch, afterwards we report what changed, and between upgrades we check the health of the network every month.

What do you get?

  • Readiness. The share of peers on the new software version, read at the handshake, and a forecast of the activation time with an 80% and a 95% band, dated and updated as activation comes closer.
  • Watch. From 24 hours before to 72 hours after activation our listener follows the chain, and an alert goes to a named person on your team when one of our signals fires.
  • Report after the upgrade. An Upgrade Report with seven fixed values under our published specification, with the raw data and the commands to count it again.
  • Monthly network health. Block intervals, the longest gaps, reorgs and peer versions every month, each against its target and dated.
  • Orphaned blocks. Blocks our listener heard that did not end up in the chain, counted per window. A count from one observation point is a lower bound, because a block that never reaches our listener cannot be counted.

Beyond upgrades we also offer TPS verification and launch measurement, under the same rules.

What have we shown so far?

Zcash NU7 (ZIP 259) activated on mainnet at block 3,543,405 on 6 November 2026, 02:34 UTC by header time, counted. Four of four published forecasts landed inside their 90% band, each scored on our track record. Our NU7 forecasts used a 90% band. From the next upgrade on we publish an 80% and a 95% band.

Our Upgrade Report PH-UR-2026-001 for it follows the same specification.

How do we stay independent?

We publish the method before anyone pays. A result is never changed for the client who paid for it. Every report names who paid. The full rules are on our independence page.

How do we start?

Write to hello@pulsehawk.io with the subject “scope for <network> upgrade”. Name the network, the upgrade and its height or date, and we reply within one business day with questions or a scope.

Questions we get asked

How long before the upgrade should measurement start?

Four weeks before activation gives a clean baseline. Shorter works too, the report then says how long the baseline was.

Do you cover the testnet activation as well?

Yes, and it is often the better test, because problems show there first. Many teams book both.

Where can we see upcoming hard forks?

In our network upgrade calendar. Every entry links to its source.
